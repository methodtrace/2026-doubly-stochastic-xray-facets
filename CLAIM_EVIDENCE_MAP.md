# Claim-to-evidence map

Every computational statement of the paper *Facets of the X-ray polytope of doubly stochastic matrices*, keyed by
the number it carries in the current preprint and the LaTeX label of the result (in square brackets; numbers
starting with SM are in the supplement), with the script that checks it, the data it reads or reproduces, and the
tier at which the stated range is reached. Printed numbers can change between builds; the labels do not, and the
code cites labels only. Words in parentheses after a script name are the names of its checks in the result file.
Paths are relative to this archive; all scripts are in `scripts/`, all tests in `tests/`.

Tiers: **quick** = `python3 run.py` (every step at its default range; under 25 minutes on 2 cores, needs pytest and
a C compiler); **certificates** = `python3 run.py --tier certificates` (verifies the shipped certificates and data
without recomputing them, about 10 minutes on 2 cores); **medium** = `python3 run.py --tier medium` (a few hours on
2 cores); **full** = `python3 run.py --tier full` or the option given (time per item on one 2.1 GHz class core unless
stated); **paper** = `python3 run.py --tier paper` (every range stated in the paper, as independent pieces; see
`README.md`). "Optional" means an option beyond the full tier; the paper tier runs every optional item listed below
except `check_facet_fibers.py --mmax 6`. Finite computations do not prove statements for all parameters; where the
paper proves a statement, the computation checks the proof or its finite input. All checks are exact (integer or
rational arithmetic, with certificates) unless marked FLOAT; FLOAT items are cross-checks, as in the paper.

Status (last column): what a reader verifies with the default tiers (quick, and certificates where stated), measured
against the range printed in the paper.

- **full stream verified**: the quick tier (or, where stated, the certificate tier) checks the claim over its whole
  printed range; for a certificate stream, every record of the shipped stream is checked.
- **small range**: the quick tier checks the claim on a smaller range than printed; the printed range needs the
  medium, full or paper tier or the optional command given in the Tier column (the full-tier counts are deposited in
  `data/summary/` and compared where the ranges overlap).
- **index only**: only the index and checksums of the certificates are checked, because the certificates are in the
  separate deposit file; with that file the status becomes "full stream verified".
- **not checked**: no script checks the statement.

has no rigid partner; E-flat holds through {7, 8} at level 15); `check_binary_increments.py` checks the binary
interval-cancellation certificate (for increments in {0, 1}, two final classes of the closure certify a ray; on all

r <= 6; `check_padding.py` recomputes the block-lift classification of the cores (`data/cores/block_lifts_kNN.json`).
These are consistent with the paper but not needed for it.
